Privacy Policy

Qoran Hadith · Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. What this tool is

Qoran Hadith is a personal tool used by its owner to publish short religious videos on the owner's own accounts. It is not a public app and has no other users.

2. Information we handle

When the owner connects a TikTok account, the tool receives an authorization token from TikTok that allows it to upload videos to that account. The token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is used only to publish videos. We do not collect personal information about viewers, followers or any other TikTok users.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share information with third parties. Videos are sent only to TikTok (and other platforms the owner chooses) in order to be published.

4. Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time from the TikTok app settings (Security and permissions → Manage app permissions) and delete the stored token from the computer.

5. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
